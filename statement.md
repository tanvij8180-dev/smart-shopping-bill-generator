# Problem Statement
## Smart Shopping Bill Generator

In shopping and retail stores, calculating the total bill manually can take time and may lead to calculation errors.

The **Smart Shopping Bill Generator** is a Python-based project designed to simplify the billing process.

The program allows the user to enter shopping item details such as item name, quantity, and price. It then calculates the item-wise amount, subtotal, discount, GST, and final bill amount.

## Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- To automate basic shopping bill calculations.
- To calculate item-wise totals accurately.
- To calculate the subtotal.
- To apply a discount.
- To calculate GST.
- To generate the final bill amount.
- To provide a simple and user-friendly billing system.

## Expected Result

The program generates a clear and properly formatted shopping bill containing the item detail , subtotal, discount, GST ,and final total
